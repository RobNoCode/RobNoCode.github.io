Kids Programme · Age 4+

Helping children build confidence, discipline & resilience

Fun, structured Brazilian Jiu Jitsu classes for children aged 4 to 13 in Peterborough. Beginner friendly, safe environment, free trial available.

Confidence starts here.

Whether your child is shy or full of energy, the mat teaches the same lesson either way: how to listen, how to try, and how to keep going.

Why families choose Impulse

Safe & structured environment

Classes designed to help children learn in a positive and supportive atmosphere.

Build confidence

We help shy children come out of their shell, and energetic children channel their energy positively.

Improve focus & discipline

Brazilian Jiu Jitsu teaches listening skills, patience, respect and self-control.

Make friends & belong

Children become part of a strong and encouraging team environment.

Real self-defence

Kids learn practical skills that help them feel calmer and more confident, built through Brazilian Jiu Jitsu technique.

Experienced coaches

Our coaches are passionate about helping children grow both on and off the mats.

Our classes

A kids programme from age 4 to 13, split by age and experience.

Tots BJJ

Ages 4–5. First steps on the mat: movement, listening skills and fun introductions to Jiu Jitsu.

Kids 1 BJJ

Ages 6–8. Building on the basics with structured technique and positional control.

Kids 2 BJJ

Ages 9+. More advanced technique, live drilling and preparation for older-age training.
