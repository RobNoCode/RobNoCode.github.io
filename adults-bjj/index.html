Our adult classes

We offer classes for complete beginners, intermediate students and advanced members, all mixed, all welcoming.

Beginners BJJ

Perfect for anyone starting for the first time. Fundamentals, movement and basic grappling concepts, taught from scratch.

Gi Classes

Traditional Brazilian Jiu Jitsu training using the kimono: grips, control and classical technique.

No-Gi Classes

Fast-paced, grip-free grappling: control, pressure and submissions used in modern competition and MMA.

Open Mat / Drills

Extra time to practise, improve and ask questions in a relaxed, self-directed session.

Not sure whether to start with Gi or No-Gi? Read our guide to Gi vs No-Gi.
