Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you're wondering before your first class

The questions we hear most from new members in Peterborough, in plain answers. If yours isn't here, get in touch directly.

GETTING STARTED

Do I need any experience to start Brazilian Jiu Jitsu?

No. Most of our members had never trained before their first class. Every beginner starts at the same point, and our coaches build sessions around fundamentals first, so you're never thrown into anything you're not ready for.

What should I wear to my first class?

Just come in comfortable gym clothes (a t-shirt and shorts or joggers) for your first session. You don't need a gi (kimono) to try a class. If you decide to continue training, we'll talk you through what to get and where.

Is the free trial class really free?

Yes. The free trial class is available to new members only, with no obligation to join afterwards. Get in touch via WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or email to book a time.

I'm unfit and have never trained. Can I still start?

Yes. BJJ is one of the more forgiving martial arts to start unfit in, since technique and pacing matter more than raw fitness early on. Fitness, strength and mobility build naturally through training rather than being a prerequisite to walk in the door.

How often should I train as a beginner?

Twice a week is a solid starting pace: enough to build momentum and let techniques sink in between sessions, without risking burnout in your first few months. You can always add more sessions once training becomes a habit.

FOR KIDS

What age can my child start Brazilian Jiu Jitsu?

Our kids programme starts at age 4 with our Tots class (ages 4 to 5), followed by Kids 1 (ages 6 to 8) and Kids 2 (ages 9 and up). Classes run Monday to Thursday.

Is Brazilian Jiu Jitsu safe for kids?

Yes. Kids classes are taught separately from adult classes, in age-appropriate groups, with a strong focus on discipline, control and safe partner work before any live sparring is introduced. Coaches are experienced black, purple and blue belts who work with children specifically, not just adults.

CLASSES & TRAINING

What's the difference between Gi and No-Gi classes?

Gi classes use the traditional kimono, with grips on the fabric as part of the technique. No-Gi classes are grip-free grappling in rash guards and shorts, which is faster paced and closer to what's used in MMA and modern grappling competitions. We run both.

How does BJJ belt grading work?

Adults move through white, blue, purple, brown and black belts, each with up to four stripes marking progress along the way. Kids follow a separate belt system. Full breakdown in our BJJ belt system guide.

Is Brazilian Jiu Jitsu effective for self-defence?

Yes. BJJ is built around control and leverage rather than striking, which makes it genuinely effective for smaller or less experienced people in a real confrontation. It's one of the reasons law enforcement and military units around the world train it specifically for control tactics.

Do you run women-only classes?

Yes, our Women's BJJ class runs Tuesday and Thursday at 19:15, led by L'neicha Betts, one of the very few female black belts in Cambridgeshire. Women are also very welcome in every mixed adult class on the timetable.
